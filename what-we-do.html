WHAT WE DO

Our Services & Early Intervention

MOHOR is committed to providing specialized care and training to children with special needs. Through in-house therapy programs, outpatient clinics, and community support, we help children realize their full potential.

What we do for our kids with special needs

Family support

Counselor-led guidance, behavior modification, and educational training to assist parents and families to cope and help children thrive at home.

Health benefits

Comprehensive diagnostics and care, including ENT, Pediatrics, Dermatology, and Orthopedics via our outpatient clinics.

Education

Tailored special education, speech therapy, and language training for children with HI, MR, Autism, ADHD, and CP.

Basic amenities

A structured, nurturing environment equipped with residential facilities and specialized aids for all-round development.

Therapy

Therapeutic sessions including Occupational Therapy (OT), Physical Therapy (PT), music, art & craft, and yoga.

Public outreach

Awareness camps, community workshops, and teacher training to build an inclusive and barrier-free society.

Our Core Motivation & Mission

Objectives of the Institute

The objective of the Institute is to assure maximum different skills to early identified children (3 months to 18 years) through the special training in different skilled areas to help them developing their verbal communication, cognitive, academic, and vocational skills for streaming into the society.

  • a) To promote various activities for prevention, identification, and early intervention of persons with special needs.
  • b) To provide education, therapeutic intervention and vocational training to special needs children for rehabilitation.
  • c) To promote residential facility for special needs (MR & CP) children.
  • d) To promote activities towards good oral aural communication of the hearing impaired children.
  • e) To organize training for teachers, therapists, parents, and community workers.
  • f) To disseminate knowledge and awareness related to various aspects pertaining to persons with special needs.
  • g) To promote equal opportunities, protection of rights, and barrier-free inclusive society.
  • h) To run sponsorship programmes for poor students, and distribution of aids and appliances.
  • i) To support public interest and charitable activities undertaken by social organizations.
  • j) To organize program/activities towards the cause as conceived from time to time.
