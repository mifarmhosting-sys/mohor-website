Leadership team

Meet the people bringing decades of experience in education, business, and social impact to the work of building brighter futures.

BA

Banmali Agrawala

Chairman, Pratham Education Foundation

Mr. Banmali Agrawala is a veteran in the energy domain. With a diverse and global experience spanning over three decades, he has spearheaded national and international organisations. Currently, he is the President, Infrastructure and Defence & Aerospace at Tata Sons Private Limited.

MC

Dr. Madhav Chavan

Co-Founder, Pratham Education Foundation

Dr. Madhav Chavan co-founded Pratham to address the problems of universal primary education in Mumbai. Since then, he has led the development of the organisation, pioneering large-scale innovations to address literacy challenges among India's underserved children.

DD

Dinyar S. Devitre

Vice Chairman, Pratham Education Foundation

Mr. Dinyar (Dinny) S. Devitre is an international business executive who has held leadership positions over a 45-year period in the consumer products and financial services industries in Asia, Australia, Japan, Europe, and the U.S. Mr. Devitre held senior operating positions with Philip Morris Inc. and then with Altria Group Inc.

JD

Jalaj Dani

Director, Reliance Foundation

Mr. Jalaj Dani is currently the Director of Reliance Foundation. He is also co-promoter of Asian Paints and has spent over two decades in leadership positions within the organisation. Throughout his career, Mr. Dani has worked extensively in healthcare, education, rural transformation, sports, skilling, and disaster management.
