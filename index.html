OUR STORY & HISTORY

Bringing opportunities to special kids

A nation's progress is not judged by its wealth but by how well its underprivileged section of people are taken care of. We take everything in life for granted and seldom are we contented with whatever we have been gifted with. If we ponder over what we do every day from dawn to dusk, we can hardly find ourselves thankful for the basic things we have. This is because we are thankful only when we achieve anything other than the ordinary.

MOHOR, as the name suggests, stands for "Gold Coin" which is considered to be precious even by the wealthiest and the most impoverished. MOHOR is much more than a symbol of riches — broadly, it synonyms the sense of security. We at MOHOR are striving to make our lives something more meaningful than just living it every day — by bringing in differences from tears to joy, from failures to successes, even if the magnitude is minute. Our efforts are directed to create extraordinary opportunities for below ordinary kids, to rehabilitate them and bring them as close as possible to normal social lives.

Our Service Offerings

In order to fulfill our objective and mission, we offer extensive services to early identified children (3 months to 18 years):

A

In-House Services

  • Assessment & programming of HI, MR, Autism, ADHD, CP
  • Multiple Disability Counseling & Behaviour modification
  • Educational guidance & Speech therapy
  • Occupational therapy & Language training
  • Creative development (Music, Art & Craft, Yoga)
B

Out-Patient Department (OPD)

  • ENT, Pediatric, Skin, & Orthopedic services

  • Hearing aid trials & fittings

  • Psychological & developmental assessments

OUR OBJECTIVES

Objectives of the Institute

The objective of the Institute is to assure maximum different skills to early identified children (3 months to 18 years) through the special training in different skilled areas to help them developing their verbal communication, cognitive, academic, and vocational skills for streaming into the society.

Prevention & Intervention

To promote various activities for prevention, identification, and early intervention of persons with special needs.

Rehabilitation & Education

To provide education, therapeutic intervention, and vocational training to special needs children for rehabilitation.

Residential Facility

To promote residential facility for special needs (MR & CP) children to ensure a safe, nurturing environment.

Oral & Aural Training

To promote activities towards good oral aural communication of the hearing impaired children.

Manpower Development

To organize training for teachers, therapists, parents, and community workers to develop experienced manpower.

Knowledge Dissemination

To disseminate knowledge and awareness related to various aspects pertaining to persons with special needs.

Rights & Advocacy

To promote activities towards equal opportunities, protection of rights, and establishing an inclusive barrier-free society.

Student Sponsorships

To run sponsorship programmes for poor students, distribution of aids and appliances according to their needs.

Charitable Collaboration

To support and participate in public interest activities of a charitable nature undertaken by missions and other social organizations.

Future Initiatives

To organize any other programmes towards the welfare and betterment of the special needs community as conceived from time to time.

WHAT WE DO

Some services we provide for our children

MOHOR offers standard, structured procedures for early identification, intervention, and vocational training for differently-abled children in Kolkata.

Family support

Counselor-led guidance, behavior modification, and educational training to assist parents and families.

Health benefits

Outpatient clinics covering ENT, Pediatrics, Dermatology, and Orthopedics to ensure holistic wellbeing.

Scholarships

Active sponsorship programs for underprivileged students and free distribution of critical aids and appliances.

Therapy

Comprehensive speech, occupational, physical, and sensory therapy, alongside creative arts, crafts, and yoga.
