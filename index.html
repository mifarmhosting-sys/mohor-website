OUR STORY & HISTORY

About MOHOR

MOHOR is a Kolkata-based non-governmental organization and special education and rehabilitation center dedicated to supporting, rehabilitating, and empowering children with special needs. Named after the Bengali word for a precious gold coin, MOHOR represents the intrinsic worth of every child, regardless of ability or socioeconomic background.

The organization operates on the core belief that society’s progress is defined by how well it cares for its most vulnerable populations. MOHOR bridges the gap between potential and opportunity, guiding differently-abled children from isolation to inclusion, and from struggle to self-reliance.

Our Services

We are dedicated to supporting children with special needs and developmental disabilities (including Autism, ADHD, Cerebral Palsy, Intellectual Disability, and Hearing Impairment).

Early Identification & Intervention

To promote activities for early detection and assessment of developmental delays in children (from 3 months to 18 years).

Read more

Therapeutic Rehabilitation

To provide specialized therapies—including Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Language Training, and Behavioral Modification—to help children acquire functional life skills.

Read more

Inclusive Education & Skill Development

To offer tailored education, cognitive training, and basic vocational guidance aimed at mainstreaming differently-abled children into regular social and academic environments.

Read more

Residential Facilities

To establish safe, nurturing residential and shelter facilities for children requiring long-term care and supervision.

Read more

Family & Community Support

To guide parents through counseling, conduct awareness drives, train caregivers and special educators, and distribute assistive aids or scholarships to underprivileged families.

Read more

Rights & Advocacy

To promote equal opportunities, protect the rights of persons with disabilities, and help establish an inclusive, barrier-free society.

Read more

Oral & Aural Training

To promote activities that support effective oral and aural communication for children with hearing impairments.

Read more

Knowledge Dissemination

To share knowledge and raise awareness about issues affecting persons with special needs.

Read more

Training

To organize training for teachers, therapists, parents, and community workers.

WHAT WE DO

Some services we provide for our children

MOHOR offers standard, structured procedures for early identification, intervention, and vocational training for differently-abled children in Kolkata.

Family support

Counselor-led guidance, behavior modification, and educational training to assist parents and families.

Health benefits

Outpatient clinics covering ENT, Pediatrics, Dermatology, and Orthopedics to ensure holistic wellbeing.

Scholarships

Active sponsorship programs for underprivileged students and free distribution of critical aids and appliances.

Therapy

Comprehensive speech, occupational, physical, and sensory therapy, alongside creative arts, crafts, and yoga.
