About MOHOR Special Education

MOHOR is a non-political and non-profitable organization solely committed to helping and aiding differently-abled children so that they can live a better life in this ever-growing and dynamic society.

We establish standard procedures to find ways and means for the prevention, early identification, and intervention of special needs. Blessed with residential facilities and organized training for teachers, parents, and relatives, our efforts help these children live independently as active participants in our society.

OUR MISSION

We make sure to provide inclusive care for children with special needs

Our mission is to promote various activities for prevention, identification and early intervention of special needs, offering residential support and professional training for teachers and families.

OUR VISION

Provide more inclusive care to children around the world

Our vision is to assure maximum different skills to early identified children (3 months to 18 years) through specialized training in verbal, cognitive, academic, and vocational areas, facilitating their stream into society.

OUR JOURNEY

Our Journey & Growth

From a modest beginning conceived by a few compassionate founders in Kasba, Kolkata, MOHOR has grown to become a cornerstone of hope and security for families with special needs children, establishing custom therapeutic pipelines and outpatient clinics.
