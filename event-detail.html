A day with our wonderful children

Opp Opolo round about, Yenagoa, Bayelsa, Nigeria
April 13, 2026 8:30 AM

About

The special visit of former Indian cricket captain Saurav Ganguly to MOHOR was a historic and joy-filled day for our children, staff, and volunteers. He spent quality time interacting with the children, understanding their daily rehabilitation programs, and appreciating their resilience. His presence brought immense happiness, reminding us all of the power of community support.

During the event, Saurav Ganguly lit the ceremonial lamp and delivered an inspiring speech emphasizing that every child deserves an equal opportunity to thrive. He observed the kids engaged in various activities like music, art, and speech training, and commended the dedication of the teachers and therapists who work tirelessly to help them integrate into mainstream society.

We are extremely grateful for the support and encouragement from our donors, guests, and patrons who made this event a success. MOHOR continues to work towards prevention, early identification, and intervention of special needs, and such events strengthen our resolve to make a meaningful difference in the lives of these children.
